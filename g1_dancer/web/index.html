Connections & settings

Connect this device to the G1 hotspot. Your music plays on its paired speaker.

Service startup

The controller service must be running on the development PC. Enable it once to start automatically:

systemctl --user enable --now g1-dancer.service

From your Mac, you can also start it with:

ssh unitree@10.42.0.1 systemctl --user start g1-dancer.service
